Nik and Ash Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Nik and Ash is a vocabulary learning app. The app does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, or share personal information, learning answers, saved words, or progress. It contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Your answers, stars, saved words, and onboarding preference are stored in local app storage on your device. The app does not sync this data to a server. Device backups, if enabled, are controlled by your operating system and account settings. You can clear this information by choosing “Start over” in Progress or uninstalling the app.

The app's learning content and character art are bundled with the app and work offline. The optional Listen control calls the operating system's speech synthesis service; availability and offline behavior depend on the device. The app does not send text to an external speech service.

If you access the companion website, its hosting provider may process standard technical request information to deliver and protect the site. This is separate from the app's locally stored learning progress.

For questions or requests, use the project support page.
